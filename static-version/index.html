The Papparoti ritual

Not just a bun

That first crack
of coffee crust.

Born in Malaysia, Papparoti began with a simple idea: a coffee-coated bun with a crisp, aromatic top and a soft, buttery middle. The contrast is what makes the first bite memorable.

Today, the signature bun brings people together across the world. Pair yours with a drink, bring a friend, or make it a quiet moment of your own.
